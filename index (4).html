Up to Tibidabo

Out of Gràcia and up, ending at the Temple Expiatori del Sagrat Cor with the whole city and the Mediterranean below. Four hours round trip, so we start at midday and are back by four.

Trainers, not sandals. Water for everyone, snacks in a bag, and sun cream — there is very little shade on the way up.

Cooking for Ten

The Feasts

Five meals in the house, all built for ten people, one oven and a holiday kitchen. Nothing here needs a skill anyone has to learn.

Read this before Thursday

Friday is La Diada. The shops will be shut.

11 September is Catalonia's national day. Supermarkets, banks and most shops close for the whole day and bakeries shut by 2pm, so everything we eat on Friday has to be in the house by Thursday night.

Do the big shop on Thursday afternoon, or book a Mercadona delivery to the house for around 5pm so nobody carries bags across town in September heat.

The Kombucha Spritz

The drink the scarves are named after. Zero alcohol as standard so everyone can have one, with a splash of gin or cava for anyone who wants it. Make a jug, keep it in the fridge, refill all weekend.

  • Ginger or hibiscus kombucha, properly cold
  • A splash of soda water
  • A squeeze of fresh orange
  • A sprig of rosemary, slapped between your hands first
  • Big ice, tall glass, orange wheel on top
  • Optional: a measure of gin, or top with cava
